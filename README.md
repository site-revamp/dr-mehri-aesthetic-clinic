# Dr. Mehri Aesthetic Clinic – Homepage Redesign

## 1. Website Overview

**Dr. Mehri Aesthetic Clinic** is a luxury medical aesthetics clinic located in Jumeirah 3, Dubai, UAE, founded by Dr. Mehri Pourkand. The clinic specialises in facial rejuvenation, skin treatments, non-surgical aesthetic procedures and body contouring.

The redesigned homepage focuses on presenting the clinic's personalised approach to aesthetic care, highlighting its treatment offerings and strengthening its professional credibility. The design direction combines a refined luxury aesthetic with a clearer content structure and a more consultation-focused user experience.

## 2. Main Improvements

The redesign addresses three key issues identified on the original website:

### 1. Generic Homepage Messaging
- Replaced the broad headline, "Rediscover Your Confidence & Beauty," with more purposeful messaging that communicates the clinic's aesthetic expertise and personalised treatment approach.
- Strengthened the homepage introduction to establish the clinic's positioning more clearly.
- Made the primary messaging more relevant to visitors exploring professional aesthetic treatments.

### 2. Limited Treatment Information
- Expanded the presentation of Face, Skin and Body treatment categories.
- Improved the organisation of treatment information to make individual service areas easier to understand.
- Created a clearer content hierarchy to help visitors identify relevant treatments and explore their options.

### 3. Fragmented Trust and Credibility
- Brought Dr. Mehri's professional profile and clinic credibility elements into a more structured presentation.
- Improved the placement and organisation of experience, recognition and professional information.
- Created a more coherent trust-building journey to support informed consultation decisions.

## 3. Design Improvements

- **Visual Presentation:** Introduced a refined luxury aesthetic appropriate for a premium medical aesthetics clinic.
- **Layout and Structure:** Improved section organisation and content hierarchy to make the homepage easier to navigate.
- **User Experience:** Created a clearer journey from the clinic introduction through treatment categories and credibility information.
- **Content Consistency:** Established a more cohesive presentation of treatment services, professional information and brand messaging.
- **Overall Appearance:** Improved the visual balance and presentation of the homepage to create a more polished and professional clinic identity.

## Project Summary

The redesigned homepage addresses the three main weaknesses of the original website: generic messaging, limited treatment information and fragmented credibility elements. The result is a more structured, informative and professionally presented homepage that better communicates Dr. Mehri Aesthetic Clinic's luxury positioning and personalised aesthetic care approach.
